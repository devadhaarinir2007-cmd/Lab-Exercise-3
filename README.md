### AIM:

To perform a Heuristic Evaluation of existing grocery delivery applications by studying and comparing Blinkit and Zepto, identifying their usability strengths and weaknesses, and applying the findings to improve the UI/UX design of a proposed grocery delivery application named QuickCart.

### ALGORITHM:
1. Select and Compare 2 Competitor Apps

Selected competitors: Blinkit and Zepto.

Compared Blinkit and Zepto with the proposed grocery application, QuickCart.
Observed their interface design, navigation, color usage, product layout, search, categories, and checkout flow.
Blinkit provides a quick and simple grocery-ordering experience with strong emphasis on fast delivery.
Zepto provides easy product discovery through categories, search, offers, and a clean shopping interface.
Identified areas where QuickCart can improve navigation, product visibility, and overall user flow.
2. Perform Heuristic Evaluation

Blinkit, Zepto, and QuickCart were evaluated using common usability principles.

The following areas were checked:

Visibility of system status
Consistency and standards
User control and freedom
Error prevention
Recognition rather than recall
Flexibility and efficiency
Visual hierarchy
Feedback and usability

Blinkit:

Simple and easy-to-understand navigation.
Clear product categories and search options.
Fast access to frequently purchased products.
Clear cart and order information.

Zepto:

Attractive and organized interface.
Good visibility of categories and offers.
Easy product search and browsing.
Clear product cards and pricing information.

QuickCart:

Needs better visual hierarchy on the home page.
Product categories should be easier to identify.
Search and cart options should be more prominent.
Checkout flow should be simple and easy to understand.
3. Analyze and Document Findings

The comparison and heuristic evaluation findings were documented.

Feature	Blinkit	Zepto	QuickCart
Navigation	Simple	Simple and organized	Needs improvement
Search	Easy to access	Easy to access	Should be more prominent
Categories	Clearly visible	Well organized	Needs better visibility
Product Cards	Clear	Attractive	Can be improved
Offers	Clearly displayed	Clearly displayed	Should be highlighted
Cart	Easy access	Easy access	Should be more visible
Checkout	Simple	Simple	Needs a smoother flow
Visual Hierarchy	Good	Good	Needs improvement
User Feedback	Clear	Clear	Can be improved
4. Propose and Apply Improvements

Based on the evaluation of Blinkit and Zepto, the following improvements were proposed for QuickCart:

Simplified the navigation menu.
Made the search bar more prominent.
Added quick-access grocery categories.
Improved product card layout.
Made prices and offers clearly visible.
Improved the placement of “Add to Cart” buttons.
Made the cart icon easier to identify.
Used better color contrast.
Improved font readability.
Simplified the checkout process.
Added clear feedback when products are added to the cart.
Maintained consistent buttons, icons, spacing, and typography.
HEURISTIC EVALUATION FINDINGS
Blinkit – Strengths
Simple home page.
Easy product search.
Quick category access.
Clear pricing and offers.
Easy cart management.
Fast and straightforward ordering flow.
Blinkit – Areas for Improvement
Large amount of promotional content can sometimes make the interface feel crowded.
Multiple categories and offers may require careful visual organization.
Zepto – Strengths
Clean and attractive interface.
Well-organized product categories.
Clear product images and prices.
Easy search and filtering.
Good visibility of offers.
Simple shopping flow.
Zepto – Areas for Improvement
Multiple promotional sections can compete for user attention.
The large number of products and categories needs clear hierarchy.
IMPROVEMENTS FOR QUICKCART

#### The following improvements were applied based on the competitor analysis:

Before Improvement:

Less visible categories.
Basic product layout.
Less prominent search option.
Complex navigation.

After Improvement:

Clear category shortcuts.
Improved product cards.
Prominent search bar.
Clearly visible cart and Add to Cart buttons.
Better color contrast.
Simple navigation.
Improved visual hierarchy.
